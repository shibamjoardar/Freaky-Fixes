Why Freaky Fixes

Mental well-being deserves the same attention as physical health. Stress, anxiety, low mood, and loneliness are common — and talking to someone trained to listen can make a real difference.

We connect you with experienced, compassionate psychologists in a space built for privacy and trust, whether you're looking for ongoing one-on-one counseling, a group session, or someone to talk to right now.

Services

  • One-on-one counseling

    Private sessions matched to what you're working through, with a psychologist you choose and can switch if it's not the right fit.

  • Group therapy

    Shared sessions with others navigating similar experiences, run by a licensed facilitator in a structured, confidential setting.

  • 24/7 support chat

    Someone to talk to, any hour you need it — through our AI chat for immediate support, or a message to the team for anything else.

Common questions

Is this confidential?

Yes. What you share with a psychologist stays between you and them, with the same confidentiality standards as in-person therapy.

How much does a session cost?

Pricing varies by psychologist and session type. Reach out through the contact form and we'll share options that fit your budget.

Can I switch psychologists if it's not a good fit?

Yes — finding the right fit matters more than sticking with a first match. Just let us know and we'll help you switch.

Do you support regional languages?

Many of our psychologists offer sessions in Bengali and Hindi as well as English — let us know your preference when you reach out.

Need to talk right now?

Our AI chat is available any time, for immediate support while you wait to connect with a psychologist. It's a starting point, not a replacement for a session — open it any time from the chat button in the corner of the screen.
